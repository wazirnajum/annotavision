1. Privacy Policy

AnotaVision ("we", "our", "us") provides data annotation services for AI, machine learning and computer vision applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details you provide through our forms, such as your name, email address, company name and message.
  • Project data you share with us for the purpose of annotation services, handled under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Usage data such as browser type, pages visited and time spent on our website, collected through standard analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested quotes or services.
  • Deliver, maintain and improve our annotation services.
  • Communicate updates, invoices and project-related information.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security & Confidentiality

We take data security seriously. All client project data is stored on access-controlled systems, annotators sign confidentiality agreements, and data is deleted or returned upon project completion in line with your agreement with us. Contact form submissions are transmitted securely and delivered to our team's inbox.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our pages. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Terms of Service

By using AnotaVision's website or engaging our annotation services, you agree to the following terms:

  • Services are provided on a project basis as agreed in a signed statement of work or contract.
  • Clients are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights to share data submitted for annotation.
  • Deliverables are provided in the agreed format and timeline; delays caused by incomplete data or scope changes will be communicated promptly.
  • All content on this website, including text, graphics and logos, is the property of AnotaVision and may not be reproduced without permission.
  • These terms may be updated periodically; continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.
